# Proof-first admission for every proposal

**Rule for all proposal kinds.** A proposal can be captured, drafted, tested and evaluated while proofs are pending. To cross into accepted, selected, activated or published state, the authoritative entry point must validate a Lean-checked admission rule for the exact enabled profile and check that this proposal satisfies its premises. Missing proof evidence returns Blocked. No UI, CLI, RPC, hook, plugin, import, restore, batch or privileged convenience path can bypass the rule.

This floor applies to factual additions/corrections, citation successor bindings, accepted designs, skill/procedure changes, learned improvements and policy/schema/rule changes. Extend the same check to any future proposal kind. Unknown kind or profile fails closed. Qualifying reusable admission rules can precede a specific proposal. Each proposal requires a Lean-kernel-checked instance certificate for its exact material inputs and witness. A proved general rule alone cannot admit an instance. A proof worker can generate the specialization automatically for routine data proposals. A fresh bespoke theorem is required when the proposal introduces a new semantic guarantee or rule beyond the proved profile.

Establish the initial release-qualified verifier/profile from an explicit operator trust root and independently built, reviewed proof artifacts. A proposed rule cannot authorize its own verification mechanism. A rule/policy change must be admitted under the current trusted rule and must also qualify the successor before activation. Keep previous semantic/encoding domains intact for authenticating historical objects.

WF33 qualifies the corresponding production Rust checks, negative paths, local storage bindings and receipt validation. Synthetic fixtures and dry-run evaluations may be exercised in isolation beforehand. The global WF31 aggregate remains final qualification at P11. Early implementation does not depend on an aggregate containing future work.

## What is checked per proposal

1. Determine the closed ProposalKind and required rule profile from trusted engine/policy data. Construct the applicable prerequisite set. A candidate cannot remove required obligations by declaring a smaller set.
2. Bind exact candidate-material digest, source/evidence closure, intended use, actor, context, expected predecessor, rule/semantic/profile versions, policy and required gates. Preserve the canonical signed proposal identity. Do not hash a future result back into its own input.
3. Verify the release-qualified proof bundle's provenance and completeness. It refers to Lean theorem symbols and statement/source/toolchain digests, checked assumptions, kernel/production compatibility profile and exact successful audit/build receipts. Bind the new proof-policy input in a versioned signed outer admission envelope. Unsigned additions to old signed plans cannot change their meaning.
4. Have the pinned Lean worker check a closed application of the rule to this proposal's exact subject, base, declared use, witness and captured premises. Require the authenticated instance receipt as well as the reusable rule bundle. Run the qualified executable witness checker and all ordinary context, freshness, correction, authorization, independent-review and empirical-evaluation gates that apply. Record the specific witness/decision and its dependencies.
5. Retain the verified closure. Recheck the active profile, authorization, policy, evidence and expected predecessor at the publication or authored-design transition boundary. Use the existing conditional mutation. A changed material input or policy creates a new operation requiring a fresh applicability decision.
6. Emit a receipt identifying the exact proposal, theorem/profile, matched witness, proof/implementation receipts, authority and selected successor. On rejection the selected state is unchanged. Pending/rejected records remain inspectable without making them usable evidence.

Classify reasons as MissingProof, UnqualifiedChecker, WrongProfile, IncompleteClosure, UnprovedNewRule, StaleWitness, ApprovalMissing, PredecessorConflict or UnsupportedProposalKind. Keep access denial distinct from disclosed existence. Historical replay checks the original rule and approval against their historical premises. A new forward selection or promotion uses the current governing profile and policy.

## The per-proposal Lean certificate

The target proposition is admissibility of the canonical proposal at its named predecessor under its named profile and recorded premises. The worker reconstructs the canonical subject and witness using qualified codecs. It checks the theorem application with Lean's kernel, records the declaration and dependency hashes, and authenticates the receipt through an independently qualified producer. Accept only controlled generated proof inputs and audited dependencies. A producer-supplied status string or an arbitrary Lean file supplied by the candidate is insufficient.

For routine instances, apply an already proved soundness theorem to the checked witness and a verified result of its decision procedure. This requires a checked specialization for each distinct material proposal but normally no new handwritten proof. For a new rule, prove the general theorem first. The instance certificate establishes applicability under stated premises. The live adapter must separately validate external observations, credentials, grant, time bounds and current predecessor before mutation. A Lean theorem cannot certify an unobserved remote state.

Order the hashes to avoid a cycle. Freeze proposal material and evidence first. Compute the instance proof on those bytes. Bind the instance receipt and rule-bundle identity in the signed outer admission intent afterward. Verify the resulting release/target separately. Cache a previously checked certificate only for the same exact subject, witness, profile, predecessor, assumptions and admissible validity conditions. A changed dependency triggers a new instance check. If the worker is unavailable and no applicable retained instance exists, the proposal stays blocked.

Minimum worker recipe on the qualified host: use an isolated clean build directory, record the actual source/tree hash and locked Lean/toolchain inputs, run the project's pinned `lake build`, then run a generated audit module through `lake env lean`. The audit references every required theorem and the proposal-instance declaration, reports their transitive axioms, and checks the allowed assumption inventory. Retain full commands, logs, exit statuses, statement identities, non-vacuity cases and the independently checked receipt. Reject solver timeout, missing declaration, unaudited axioms or a changed prover/checker profile. Lean can run on a separate local/CI proof worker while the distributed Rust artifact validates the authenticated receipt and actual premises.

## Minimum profile matrix

| Proposal | Mathematical and executable preconditions | Additional evidence required at the boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fact, correction or citation binding | Applicable T01-T22 judgments plus the CIT profile, checked source binding and context assignment | Exact retained evidence, current applicable policy and matching authenticated approval |
| Accepted design or implementation binding | Relevant workflow-3 design/portfolio rules, V12/V13 and applicable authored lifecycle protocol | Exact design and intended source, actor/readiness checks and correct target binding when required |
| Procedure, skill or learned patch | Applicable engineering E targets, package/effect typing, capability and gate-floor preservation, learning admission when relevant | Pinned resources, evaluation results, scope, counterexamples, current grants and signed selection |
| Changed policy, schema or semantic rule | Admission under old floor and independent proofs of new rule/profile, migration and compatibility | Retained old/new policy diff, mapping inventory, affected cases, authority and rollback/selection contract |
| Future unregistered kind | No qualifying profile | Remains a candidate until a qualified kind, validator and transition mapping exist |

Treat the matrix as a minimum. Freeze an exact, reviewed mapping from each enabled kind and transition to theorem IDs, callers, protocol actions and verifier profile before shipping it. All release-time and per-proposal receipts must be linked through content and provenance. The runtime can consume an authenticated pre-built proof bundle and verified checker without shipping Lean to every workstation. A theorem name or a candidate-supplied `proof_passed` flag is insufficient.

Formal guarantees here concern admissibility under stated assumptions. Correctness of an external report, usefulness of a new lesson, and reliability of a provider need their own evidence. Capture failed tests, contradictory observations, footprint changes, unknown outcomes and promotion attempts without erasing earlier history.

## Qualification and releases

At P03C qualify the pure common floor and citation rules. At P05C exercise the actual Rust decisions, parser/caller boundaries and proof-certificate checks in isolation. P07 connects the qualified checks and earlier publication obligations before enabling authoritative knowledge writes. P08D applies the floor to design acceptance. P18 applies it to learning promotions and brings its own E/V/M evidence before enabling that profile. A profile that lacks its required proofs continues to return Blocked regardless of other profiles' progress.

For policy/key/profile updates test replay under the wrong signing domain, expired grant, substituted proof bundle, interrupted publication, stale expected predecessor, fabricated witness and restore against another realm. Persist proof failure as failure. A missing or timed-out required check never counts as a pass. See [the priority queue](../PRIORITIES.md).
