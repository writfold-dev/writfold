# Expose a human reading channel

[[Writfold/Generations/demo-r1/Overview|Overview]] · **Demo snapshot** · Operations · 2 Nov 2026, 12:00 UTC

Present admitted knowledge as readable notes and scoped dashboards. Keep the engine's release and context identity attached to every page.

## Why

A person should be able to understand the claim and follow its support without reading raw storage records.

## Boundary

Edits to notes are not approvals. The channel publishes a view; a future import workflow would propose changes for review.
