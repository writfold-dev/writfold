# What a good reflection covers

Write it as one checkpoint entry starting `reflection:`, then attest this
gate. A good reflection is short and specific to this run:

- **Outcome.** What changed, and whether it did what the task asked. Name
  the commit or the files.
- **Surprises.** What took longer or went differently from the plan, and
  why. "Nothing" is an answer, if it is true.
- **Evidence.** Which gates or citations the result rests on, and any that
  were waived or skipped, with the reason.
- **What the KB lacked.** A question the KB could not answer during the
  run. Either propose the record that would have answered it
  (`writfold kb propose …`), or write a line starting `no proposal:` with
  the reason.
- **Next time.** One change to the plan template or the gates, if any.

Do not restate the diff, and do not grade the work: say what a reader
picking up the next run needs to know.
