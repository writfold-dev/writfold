# Research: what it means here

An example. Each heading is a question for your team; the answer under it is a
starting point. Writfold reads none of this: the agent and the people who
cover the checklist do.

## Which sources count?

The KB first (`writfold kb find`, `why`, `context`), then the code and its
tests, then outside documentation. A source is cited, not summarised from
memory.

Change this.

## What makes a precedent comparable?

The same kind of change in the same part of the system, or the same failure
seen elsewhere. Say why it is comparable, not only that it exists.

Change this.

## Reuse, extend, extract or keep it local?

None is preferred. Say which one the change does and what made the others
worse here.

Change this.

## What must a search that finds nothing record?

Where it looked (repositories, paths, queries) and where it stopped. A search
that found nothing says that the looks found nothing; it is never a claim that
nothing exists.

Change this.

## What belongs in the KB afterwards?

A decision when one was made, a link when two records turned out related, an
investigation when a question stays open. "Nothing new" is an acceptable
answer when the reason says what was checked.

Change this.

## How are open questions kept?

As an investigation with the question in it, so the next session finds it.

Change this.
