# The laws of the calculus

Generated by `proofs/laws_page.py` from `proofs/Calculus/`, `proofs/laws.toml`,
`proofs/mutants.py` and `proofs/unlinked.txt`; do not edit by hand. The quality
gate refuses this page when it differs from a fresh one.

Each law is a theorem in plain Lean ([RFC](../rfcs/2026-11-27-calculus-in-lean.md)),
independent of the Rust. A kernel is *linked* when a theorem shows the shipped
Rust, through its Aeneas translation, implements the operation a law is about;
every law of that operation then holds of the kernel. A law is *caught* by the
core mutants that break it, or *derived* from the laws it follows from.

**Rule** (AGENTS.md): new or changed decision logic states the law it obeys in
`proofs/Calculus` with a doc comment, links its kernel in `proofs/laws.toml`, and
regenerates this page in the same commit.

### `Calculus.Tri.all_append`

### `Calculus.remainder_append`

The remainder of two parts is the sum of their remainders.

```lean
remainder_append (xs ys : List Member) : remainder (xs ++ ys) = remainder xs + remainder ys
```

- Derived: List.countP_append

### `Calculus.whole_iff_no_remainder`

A universe is whole exactly when no member is left over.

```lean
whole_iff_no_remainder (ms : List Member) : whole ms = true ↔ Member.remainder ∉ ms
```

- Implemented by: `inventory.complete`, `walk.complete`
- Caught by: `remainder-counts-declared-out`, `whole-ignores-remainder`

### `Calculus.whole_append`

Two parts are whole together exactly when each is whole.

```lean
whole_append (xs ys : List Member) : whole (xs ++ ys) = (whole xs && whole ys)
```

- Implemented by: `inventory.complete`
- Derived: remainder_append

### `Calculus.universal_yes_iff`

Law U: a universal word holds exactly when the universe is whole, every judged member holds, and it is inhabited unless the empty universe was allowed.

```lean
universal_yes_iff (e : Bool) (ms : List (Member × Tri)) : universal e ms = .yes ↔ whole (ms.map (·.1)) = true ∧ (∀ t ∈ judgedOf ms, t = .yes) ∧ (e = true ∨ judgedOf ms ≠ [])
```

- Caught by: `universal-skips-whole`, `universal-empty-yes`

### `Calculus.remainder_never_whole`

```lean
remainder_never_whole (e : Bool) (ms : List (Member × Tri)) (h : Member.remainder ∈ ms.map (·.1)) : universal e ms ≠ .yes
```

- Derived: universal_yes_iff, whole_iff_no_remainder
