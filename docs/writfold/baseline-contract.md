## Frozen evaluation and explicit limits

The retained protocol is `1.0.0`, corrected corpus `1.0.2`, seed 40123: 24 **synthetic** tasks across `atlas` and `boreal`. File, existing-index and native-Dolt retrieval share pinned semantic inputs and payloads. Cold means a fresh worker, including startup/close, **not** flushed OS caches. Warm means repeated requests in the same worker. Context/source byte counts and IO counters are proxies, not model tokens or physical device IO. Worker/largest-child high-water RSS is not simultaneous process-tree peak; historical maxima must never be summed into one.

Existing acceptance ratios are frozen: Dolt/file warm p95 at most **1.25**, context bytes at most **1.25**, repeated source bytes at most **0.9**; deterministic invalid inference, citation retargeting, completeness inflation and correctness-regression allowances remain zero. These are the existing synthetic budgets, not newly measured hardware thresholds or a promise that Dolt beats the warm index.

Preserve both model attempts. Original receipts failed all 48 exact labels because evidence-record identity was absent, despite matching outcome/scope/inference fields. Corrected receipts retain baseline **23/24** and candidate **24/24**. The strict importer still failed for baseline `atlas-07`; the separate local candidate assessment is not permission to relabel that report as passing. Equal retrieved payloads do not establish a Dolt-caused reasoning improvement. Receipt imports attest external execution; this inventory did not invoke the models.

**No production corpus is supplied.** Synthetic “held-out” labels do not establish production-wide quality.
